Racktoria Privacy Policy

Effective 10/29/2026

Racktoria is a game made by Amarrack ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who handles it, and the choices you have. We don't run our own servers and we don't collect or store your personal information ourselves.

Your game save

Your company, progress and settings are saved on your device. They stay there until you start a new game or uninstall the app.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google stores your achievements, leaderboard scores and a cloud copy of your save in your Google account, so you can carry on on another device. That data is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage or delete it in your Google account and the Play Games app. We never see your Google account details beyond your Play Games profile name.

Advertising

Racktoria shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner in the game, and videos you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address (which gives a rough location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, through Google's consent message. Without consent you see non-personalised ads.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California, Virginia, Colorado and Connecticut): you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that you bought it, never your payment details. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

Racktoria is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

  • Your save on the device: start a new game, or uninstall Racktoria.
  • Play Games data: manage or delete it in your Google account.
  • Advertising data held by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID, and see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
